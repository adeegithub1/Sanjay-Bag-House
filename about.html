How Sanjay Bag House became a trusted name in Indian bags and luggage.

Sanjay Bag House began as a small family-run store with one simple belief — a bag should work as hard as the person carrying it. What started as a modest counter selling school bags and travel luggage has grown into a trusted destination for premium, everyday-ready bags across India.

Every product we sell is chosen for the same three things: durability, comfort and honest pricing. We work directly with manufacturers who share our obsession with stitching, zippers and straps that are built to survive daily use — not just look good on a shelf.

Today, thousands of students, professionals and travellers carry a Sanjay Bag House product every single day. We're proud of that trust, and we work to earn it again with every order.
