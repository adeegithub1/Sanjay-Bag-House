Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Return Eligibility

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for products that are unused, undamaged and in their original packaging with all tags intact.

How to Initiate a Return

Go to My Orders in your account, select the relevant order and click "Request Return". Our team will arrange a pickup within 2-3 business days.

Refunds

Once the returned product passes quality check, refunds are processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method. COD orders are refunded via bank transfer.

Non-Returnable Items

Products marked as "Final Sale" and items showing signs of use are not eligible for return.
