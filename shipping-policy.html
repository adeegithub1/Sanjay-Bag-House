Shipping Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Shipping Timelines

We dispatch orders within 1-2 business days of confirmation. Delivery typically takes 4-7 business days depending on your location, with metro cities receiving orders faster than remote areas.

Shipping Charges

Shipping is free on all orders above ₹999. Orders below this amount are charged a flat shipping fee of ₹79.

Order Tracking

Once your order is shipped, a tracking link will be shared via email and SMS. You can also track your order anytime from your account.

Delivery Issues

If your order is delayed or you face any delivery issue, please contact our support team at support@sanjaybaghouse.in or call +91 98765 43210.
